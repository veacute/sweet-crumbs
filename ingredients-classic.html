Classic Chocolate Brownies

A simple ingredient list for a small batch of homemade brownies.

Watch a tutorial

Ingredients

Makes one small pan. Ask an adult for help with the hot oven.

  • ½ cup melted butter
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease or line an 8-inch square pan.
  2. Mix melted butter and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla, then mix until smooth.
  4. Stir in flour, cocoa powder, and salt until just combined.
  5. Spread the batter in the pan and bake for about 20–25 minutes.
  6. Let the brownies cool before slicing.

Note: Oven temperatures and baking times can vary. Follow your oven's instructions and use adult supervision.
