Chocolate Chip Brownies

A classic brownie base with extra chocolate chips mixed into the batter.

Watch a tutorial

Ingredients

Makes one small pan. Ask an adult for help with the hot oven.

  • ½ cup melted butter
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Prepare an 8-inch square pan.
  2. Mix melted butter and sugar.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla and stir until smooth.
  4. Fold in flour, cocoa powder, salt, and chocolate chips.
  5. Spread the batter evenly in the pan.
  6. Bake for about 20–25 minutes, then cool before cutting.

Note: Oven temperatures and baking times can vary. Follow your oven's instructions and use adult supervision.
