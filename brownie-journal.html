My baking notes

A simple journal page to record what worked and what I want to try next time.

Before baking

  • Read the recipe first.
  • Measure ingredients carefully.
  • Prepare the baking pan.

While baking

  • Mix until just combined.
  • Keep an eye on baking time.
  • Use oven mitts with adult help.

After baking

  • Let the brownies cool.
  • Write down any changes.
  • Share a few pieces with family.
